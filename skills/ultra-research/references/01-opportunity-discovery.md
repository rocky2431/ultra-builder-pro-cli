# Opportunity discovery

Use for workflow step `01-opportunity-discovery`.

## Objective

Connect the validated problem to desired outcomes and evidence-backed opportunities.
Describe opportunities from the actor's perspective rather than as a feature list.

## Evidence

- Derive current outcomes from observed workflows, existing behavior, interviews, or
  owner decisions.
- Preserve causal links between a problem, an unmet outcome, and its evidence.
- Do not force rankings, solution brainstorming, or a fixed number of opportunities.
- Record opportunities that lack evidence as unknown or future research.

## Record

Update `discovery.md` with:

- desired outcomes and their evidence;
- opportunity statements and affected actors;
- constraints or dependencies;
- owner-decided priority when one exists;
- rejected or deferred opportunities only when that decision is durable.

Complete the step with the evidence used and the updated output path.

## Semantic record

Use kind `opportunity`. Record `actor`, `desired_outcome`, and `evidence_status`. Link
the record to its problem when that relation is established.
