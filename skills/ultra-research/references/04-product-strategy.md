# Product strategy

Use for workflow step `04-product-strategy`.

## Objective

Turn accepted evidence into a product direction, target boundary, value proposition,
and explicit tradeoffs.

## Evidence and decisions

- Derive constraints from prior research and current system behavior.
- Ask the owner when a scope, customer, business, or risk decision is load-bearing.
- Do not infer an MVP, reduced scope, release phase, or commercial posture.
- Record exclusions only when the owner or an accepted constraint establishes them.

## Record

Update `discovery.md` with:

- product direction and intended actors;
- value delivered through observable behavior;
- chosen tradeoffs and their rationale;
- constraints, exclusions, and unresolved decisions;
- any defensibility claim with its supporting evidence.

Complete the step with decision and evidence references plus the updated output path.

## Report trace

In the area report, record `tradeoff` and `rationale`, then link the decision file or
specification anchor to the problems, opportunities, or constraints that support it.
