# Actors and jobs

Use for workflow step `10-user-personas`.

## Objective

Describe evidence-backed actors, their jobs, operating context, goals, constraints, and
adoption barriers. A role or actor model is sufficient; do not invent demographic
personas.

## Evidence

- Prefer observed workflows, access roles, support evidence, usage data, and owner
  decisions.
- Link each actor to a real entry point or intended behavior.
- Mark unverified motivations and pain points as unknown.
- In brownfield adoption, document the actors represented by current code and runtime,
  even when product research is incomplete.

## Record

Update `product.md` with actor identity, job, context, current workflow, desired outcome,
constraints, and evidence. Record priority only when it is an accepted decision.

Complete the step with evidence references and the updated output path.

## Report trace

In the area report, record `job`, `current_workflow`, `goal`, and `constraint`, then
link the actor heading in `product.md`.
