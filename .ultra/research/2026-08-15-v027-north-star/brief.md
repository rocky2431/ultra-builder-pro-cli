# Research Brief: v0.27 North Star constitution

## Observed

- The pre-Change v0.26 template used `Project Direction`, `North Star Outcome`, and
  `Hard Constraints`, but did not define falsifiable first-principle propositions,
  revision acceptance, or a causal chain.
- The accepted construction plan requires first principles and adversarial review to
  constrain the complete six-Host lifecycle.

## Decision

Run the smallest bounded Research coverage that can distinguish project principles from
implementation preferences: `00-problem-validation`, `04-product-strategy`,
`05-assumptions-validation`, `22-success-metrics`, `41-quality-risks`, then `99-synthesis`.

## Inference

The existing repository, maintained documents, owner record, Phase 0 evidence, and
previously gathered multi-model analyses are sufficient to propose r1 without new web
claims. They are not sufficient to claim the implementation or live Host drills passed.

## Unknown

- Whether independent review materially improves defect detection remains an evaluation
  question, not a premise accepted by validator success.
- Whether every Host can satisfy the final native activation and delegation contracts
  remains future v0.27 acceptance evidence.

## North Star effect

- Effect: `supports` the need for `FP-1` through `FP-6`, `NS-01`, and `HC-1` through
  `HC-6`, subject to the selected lenses and owner acceptance.
- Starting authority: legacy v0.26, unrevisioned; no accepted v2 digest existed when the
  run began.
- Stop condition: a structurally valid r1 candidate, all selected reports, one linked
  synthesis, and a stable owner acceptance record exist without deleting legacy evidence.
