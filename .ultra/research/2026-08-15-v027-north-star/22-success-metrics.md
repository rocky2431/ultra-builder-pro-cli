## Observed

- No current telemetry justifies a numeric automation-rate or quality target.
- Task count, finding count, and green self-integrity can improve while owner outcome or
  live continuity remains broken.

## Verified

- The accepted Change defines observable acceptance across trace, autonomy, review,
  delegation, adapters, Doctor, recovery, and exact candidate identity.
- `.ultra/north-star.md#north-star-outcomes` records an observation method, baseline,
  expected change, horizon, and anti-metric for `NS-01`.

## Decided

  do not fabricate a numeric North Star metric.

- Recoverable end-to-end delivery on six Hosts is closer to delivered value than an
  automation percentage; component evidence remains diagnostic, not product meaning.

- Candidate parity and provider receipts do not exist until later tasks execute.
