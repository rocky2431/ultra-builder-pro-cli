# Decision: Accept the Ultra Builder Pro 5.0 product boundary

> **Status**: accepted
> **Decision identity**: `2026-08-25-ultra-5-0-scope`
> **Acceptance time**: `not-recorded`

## Question

Should Ultra Builder Pro 5.0 combine the owner-legible workflow repair and
cross-Agent execution transport with the earlier Team Pack / remote shared-asset
research, or keep those as separate product boundaries?

## Options and trade-offs

1. **Workflow and multi-Agent execution coherence only** — make the existing
   file-first product legible, grant-aware, recoverable, and terminal; replace the
   broken runtime delegation dependency with a Skill-local ACP-first transport;
   preserve a narrow future seam for separately governed shared assets.
2. **Also ship Team Pack and remote shared assets** — produce a broader team
   product in one release, but add publisher identity, revision review, remote
   acquisition, activation, permission, revocation, and supply-chain authority to
   an already consequential workflow and transport migration.

## Accepted decision

Select option 1. Ultra Builder Pro 5.0 includes:

- a shared Route Contract and owner-facing Route Card for the public workflow;
- clear Init, Research, Change, Plan, Status, Dev, Test, and Deliver ownership;
- grant-aware continuation that pauses only at material checkpoints;
- owner-decision revision/history and fresh-session recovery guidance;
- early observable vertical-slice guidance and real user-journey acceptance;
- a Skill-local delegation runner;
- a provider-neutral `AgentExecutionDriver` with ACP-first, conformance-gated
  `CliDriver` fallback;
- a thin ZCode transport over the installed ZCode runtime;
- 3.x-compatible migration, Doctor coverage, cross-Host verification, and local
  release-candidate packaging.

Ultra Builder Pro 5.0 does **not** include Team Pack, a remote asset registry,
shared Hook or MCP auto-activation, a Team Marketplace, or a persistent ACP
daemon. Those remain a separately governed future product decision.

## Interaction decision

Inside an accepted Change and a valid Execution Grant, every public Skill
transition first shows a Route Card. The host may continue through a covered
non-material transition. It stops for a material checkpoint: a new or reduced
product commitment, a changed Change boundary, unresolved evidence that changes
that boundary, a new or widened grant/effect/budget, a canonical-writer or
topology change, unresolved P0/P1 risk, finalization, or an external/irreversible
effect. Without a valid grant, a public Skill reports the card and recommends one
next public workflow without launching it.

## Owner record

- Conversation scope: the owner asked to use the opportunity to make Ultra 5.0
  and solve the prior workflow and delegation problems as one complete upgrade.
- Exact raw owner acceptance: 「接受」, replying to the recommended boundary and
  interaction decision immediately above in the same conversation.
- Agency boundary: the owner accepts the product outcome, material scope,
  exclusions, and checkpoint behavior. The model owns reversible decomposition,
  implementation design, evidence interpretation, and final wording inside that
  frame.
- Time boundary: the conversation supplied no authoritative acceptance time, so
  this record uses `not-recorded` and invents none.
- Effect boundary: this decision authorizes local repository implementation and
  verification. It does not authorize commit, push, tag, npm publication,
  GitHub Release, real global installation, credentials, provider spend, or any
  other external or irreversible effect.

## Consequences

- The accepted `north-star-v2-r3` constitution remains current. 5.0 projects
  `NS-01` through a versioned Route Contract rather than adding a new semantic
  state machine or provider-specific principle.
- ACP sessions and progress events are derived execution observations only.
  `.ultra` canonical files and Git remain semantic authority.
- Delegation remains a bounded worker relationship. Moving the canonical writer
  remains the mutually exclusive primary-transfer protocol.
- Existing 3.x accepted artifacts remain readable and are never rewritten merely
  because the plugin is upgraded.

## What would change this decision

A separately accepted Team Pack initiative may supersede the exclusion after a
real repeated owner need, an explicit publisher/revision/permission authority
model, and a fetch-review-activate recovery drill exist. It does not inherit the
5.0 implementation grant.
